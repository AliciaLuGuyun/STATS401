Use one hand in good light, facing the camera. Move across about one fifth of the mirrored preview within half a second. To play/pause, fold all four fingers and hold your thumb upright and still for about half a second, then release. To explore, extend only your index finger and move it across the preview; exploration pauses playback. Bring thumb and index tips together briefly to select the hovered company. The cursor freezes while pinching, so closing your fingers does not shift the target. Separate fingertips and point again before another pinch. After swiping, hold still or lower your hand to rearm. An open palm held still has no command.

Video is processed locally in your browser and is never recorded or uploaded. Starting downloads the MediaPipe runtime and model. Leaving this tab stops the camera; restart explicitly when you return.

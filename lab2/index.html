Design Justification

I used a separate row for each city so viewers can identify and compare individual observations easily. Population is represented by horizontal bar length because length makes numerical comparisons clear. Average temperature is represented by the horizontal position of a circle on a separate temperature scale. Region is encoded with color because it is a nominal category without a natural order. Development level is represented by circle size, with larger circles indicating higher development levels. Keeping population bars and temperature markers in separate aligned sections avoids comparing values that use different units on the same scale. This design allows viewers to compare population and temperature across cities while also noticing regional patterns and differences in development level.
